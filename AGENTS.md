# ZenoFit Workspace Rules & Project Direction

## True Agreed Project Goal & Target Audience (Team Consensus)
- **Core Product Goal:** ZenoFit is a seated VR active-break game designed for **lazy geeks / couch potatoes**—people who are reluctant/lazy to move and spend excessive hours in front of screens:
  - Desk/screen workers and university students (coding, remote work, study),
  - Hardcore gamers (*gamers akut*),
  - Homebodies / socially avoidant individuals (*ansos*) who prefer staying indoors and avoid gyms or exercising in public.
- **Secondary Benefit (*Curb-Cut Effect*):** Keeping every exercise 100% seated and upper-body only naturally preserves accessibility for wheelchair users and people with lower-body mobility limitations, without defining ZenoFit's primary brand or product scope.
